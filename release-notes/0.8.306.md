# 0.8.306 — Office 2016 charts from PowerPoint, drawn from their data (2026-11-05)

## Highlights
- PowerPoint's newer chart types — **waterfall, funnel, histogram, Pareto, box & whisker,
  treemap and sunburst** — now open as a crisp drawing of the chart made of shapes, in the
  deck's colours, instead of the low-resolution picture PowerPoint saves beside them.

## Features
- Each chart is drawn from the values the file saved: waterfall totals and connectors, histogram
  bins as Office makes them, Pareto's cumulative line on a 0–100% axis, box & whisker quartiles,
  whiskers, means and outliers, treemap groups with their banners, sunburst rings.
- The chart's values go into the group's **alt text**, so screen readers (and you) can read them.
- Region (map) charts, and charts saved without their values, still come in as PowerPoint's
  picture.
