# 0.8.293 — PowerPoint import for everyone; a hardening pass (2026-10-31)

## Highlights
- **Opening PowerPoint files is no longer a Dev Mode preview.** File → Open and drag-and-drop accept `.pptx` / `.potx` / `.ppsx` for everyone. Before switching it on, the importer was run against LibreOffice's collection of ~440 real-world test decks: none crashed or hung, and the differences against LibreOffice's own rendering were worked down category by category.
- **The first week of the hardening month.** Eight Playwright specs that had been "known failures" for weeks were triaged. Three turned out to be real bugs (below), the rest specs left behind by deliberate changes, and all of them pass now.

## Features
- **PowerPoint import, made public**, and now also bringing in:
  - **table styles** from the deck itself (header row, banded rows and columns, table background, borders), not only PowerPoint's default style;
  - **pattern fills** as Yappy pattern fills in the deck's colours;
  - **background pictures** laid out as PowerPoint draws them, stretched, tiled or see-through;
  - **pictures cropped to a shape** (an oval photo) as a clipping mask, and **grayscale** pictures in grey;
  - shapes set to **"use the slide background"**;
  - **SmartArt** saved without its drawing, which now keeps its text as an outline instead of coming in as an empty frame.
- **Math in the Transform fields.** X, Y, W, H and rotation in the Properties panel's Transform group take expressions like the rest of the panel: `*2`, `200-50%`, `+10`. They commit on Enter, Tab or leaving the field.

## Fixes
- **PowerPoint import:**
  - Slides turned **black** under a layout with an inverted colour map and no background of its own.
  - A placeholder filled from the theme came in **empty** (white text on white).
  - An inherited "no outline" was ignored.
- **The node tool's Delete button was out of reach at around 1200 px wide.** It was clipped under the view controls. The options row now shrinks and scrolls, and the Support button shows just its heart while a tool's options are on screen.
- **Esc clears a spotlight before it ends the presentation**, as the toolbar's tooltip says. It used to end the show and leave the spotlight on.
- **Colour contrast (WCAG AA) in the light, dark and focus themes:**
  - the Support label;
  - the active symmetry and grid-style buttons;
  - two hint lines in the Properties panel;
  - the ▴▾ stepper arrows in number fields;
  - selected buttons in dark mode, which showed a light chip.
- **Numbered lists:** the items of one level line up again after 0.8.292's wide-number fix. "1." and "2." had been measured separately.
- **Background → Detach as image** made a nearly invisible picture when the background was see-through.
- **Hotkey list:** the Pen's straight-segment snap reads 45°, as it works (it said 15°), in all five languages.

## Internal / tests / docs
- PowerPoint: a corpus harness in `temp/` (`pptx-corpus-one.ts`, `pptx-corpus-render.mjs`, `pptx-corpus-diff.mjs`). SlideModel's in-browser viewer served as a second opinion where LibreOffice and Yappy disagreed. Tests: `read.test.ts` (backgrounds, crop to shape, grayscale, table styles, SmartArt text), `to-yappy.test.ts` (patterns), `tests/pptx-import.spec.ts` (tiled background with a clipped picture; import without Dev Mode).
- Playwright:
  - `tests/_global-warmup.ts` loads the app once before the suite, so cold Vite compiles no longer time out the first spec on each worker.
  - Specs updated: undo-depth range, Pen staying active after closing, crop drag through the canvas origin, the Kaleidoscope locator.
  - The stick-figure specs wait for the canvas and poll the screen instead of sleeping.
- Help docs checked against what shipped in September–October. Eleven pages corrected, among them Pen 45°, the Width axis, auto-layout palette entries, the History panel, Export HTML paths and `playScene()`. A new **Slides & presentations** section in Design Studio.
- `docs/slides-feature-gaps.md`: a backlog of presentation features, with DeckCraft as the reference.
