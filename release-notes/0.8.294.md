# 0.8.294 — Speaker notes, presenter view, links in the show, slide layouts (2026-11-01)

## Highlights
- **Presenting got its missing half.** Each slide has speaker notes, and **Alt+F5** presents with a
  presenter view: a second window with the current and next slide, the notes, a timer and the
  clock. Notes travel to and from PowerPoint.
- **Interactive decks.** Any object can link to another slide, a show action (next, previous,
  first, last, end) or a web page, and clicking it while presenting follows the link.
- **New slide from a layout**, and Slide Master artwork with slide numbers that finally come out on
  every page of an export.
- The first four items of the DeckCraft-based backlog (`docs/slides-feature-gaps.md`).

## Features
- **Speaker notes.** A notes pane docks under the canvas (notebook button in the slide toolbar,
  or *Show / hide speaker notes* in the command palette); the slide re-fits above it. Typing is one
  undo step per session. PowerPoint export writes real notes slides (both export types) and import
  reads them; a deck with notes opens with the pane showing. Markdown import takes `Note:` lines and
  `<!-- … -->` comments; presentation templates can carry notes.
  API: `getSlideNotes`, `setSlideNotes`, `toggleSpeakerNotes`.
- **Presenter view** (Alt+F5, the presentation toolbar, or the command palette): current and next
  slide, notes with A−/A+ sizing, timer with pause/reset, clock, Previous / Next / End show; the
  show's keys work in it. Ending the show closes it. A **Full screen** button joins the
  presentation toolbar, because a browser can't open a window and go full screen on one click.
  API: `openPresenterView`, `closePresenterView`, `isPresenterViewOpen`.
- **Links.** Right-click → 🔗 Link: *To slide ▸*, *Show action ▸*, *Web address…*, *Remove link*.
  A hand cursor over linked objects while presenting; clicking follows the link. Slide links follow
  their slide through reordering. PowerPoint export and import keep them (`hlinkClick`, slide jumps,
  show jumps). API: `setLink`, `getLink`, `followLink`.
- **New slide from a layout.** A button beside + Add Slide: Blank, Title, Title and content, Two
  columns, Section header, Comparison, Quote, Key numbers, Timeline, Cards, Picture and text,
  Closing — placeholder text in your language, the current slide's background, colours picked to
  read on it, scaled to any slide size. API: `insertSlideWithLayout`, `getSlideLayouts`.
- **`${date}` field** alongside `${slideNumber}` and `${totalSlides}`.

## Fixes
- **#464 — exports dropped Slide Master artwork and printed field formulas.** A master logo or
  footer appeared on page 1 only of a PDF, PNG, SVG or PowerPoint export, and a slide-number footer
  came out as `=Page ${slideNumber} of ${totalSlides}`. Thumbnails and recordings showed the
  formula too.
- **#463 — Open Link opened any scheme.** A `javascript:` or `data:` link in a document would be
  opened. Only `http(s)`, `mailto`, `tel`, slide links and show actions are followed now.
- **#462 — slides fitted to the window, not the canvas.** In the editor a slide's left edge sat
  under the slide navigator and its bottom under the status bar; presented slides were pushed
  46 px right and clipped; and the navigator sat under the docked toolbar, hiding slide numbers.
- **#460 — black vanished from pages in the Dark theme** (white on a white slide), in slide
  thumbnails (Dark, Focus, even System on a light OS), in recordings, and in Focus while presenting.
- **#461 — the slide navigator stayed white** in Dark and Focus.
- Opening the presenter view took the editor out of full screen, which ended the show (found by its
  spec before release).
