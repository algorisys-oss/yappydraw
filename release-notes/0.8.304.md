# 0.8.304 — PowerPoint import: metafile pictures, TIFF, embedded objects, video and audio (2026-11-05)

## Highlights
- PowerPoint decks with **video and audio clips** now bring them in as playable clips, with
  PowerPoint's Start Automatically, Loop until Stopped and mute settings, and Yappy writes them
  back to PowerPoint as real clips.
- **EMF and WMF pictures** (logos and diagrams pasted from Office or Visio) come in as sharp
  vector pictures instead of placeholders. **TIFF pictures** and **embedded objects** (Excel,
  Word, equations, Visio) come in as pictures too.

## Features
- **Video and audio from PowerPoint** (`p14:media`, `a:videoFile`, `a:audioFile`,
  `a:wavAudioFile`): embedded clips are stored in the document; web links stay links. Audio shows
  its speaker icon and plays in a small player bar. WMV, AVI, MPEG, WMA and MIDI (which browsers
  can't play), clips over 25 MB or past 50 MB per presentation, and links to local files come in
  as their poster picture, with a message naming them.
- **Video and audio to PowerPoint**: video elements export as PowerPoint clips with their poster,
  autoplay, loop and mute. YouTube and Vimeo videos export as a picture linked to the video.
- **EMF / WMF / EMF+ pictures** become SVG: lines, fills, text, clipping and embedded bitmaps,
  checked against LibreOffice on 121 files.
- **TIFF pictures** are converted to PNG at import (uncompressed, LZW, Deflate, PackBits; grey,
  palette, RGB(A), CMYK). JPEG-compressed TIFF stays a placeholder.
- **Embedded (OLE) objects** come in as the preview picture PowerPoint shows for them, with their
  box and outline; the message names the object type.

## Fixes
- **#477** In the slide show, autoplay started only the first autoplay video in the whole
  document, which could be on another slide. Every autoplay clip on the current slide now starts,
  including on the slide the show opens on, and clips stop when the show ends.
- **#478** Clicking a video in the slide show advanced the slide; it now plays or pauses the video.
- **#479** An embedded video's placeholder label printed its base64 data; it shows its name.
- An imported EMF picture was labelled `image/x-emf` while holding SVG.
