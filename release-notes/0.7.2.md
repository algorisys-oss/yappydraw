# 0.7.2 — Whole-design export fix (2026-07-07)

## Highlights
- Exporting a **Design** (or multi-page **Slides**) document to PNG, JPG or SVG
  now captures the *entire* design — every page at its full bounds, with its own
  background — instead of a tight crop around the elements.

## Features
- None.

## Fixes
- **Design/Slides export was cropped to elements.** Downloading a Canva-style
  design as PNG/JPG/SVG produced an image cropped to the element bounding box on
  a flat white background — the page background (colour/gradient/texture) and any
  empty margin were missing, and multi-page designs came out as a jumbled
  element box. Whole-document PNG/JPG export of a paged doc now renders every
  page at its exact page bounds with its own background, stacked vertically with
  a gap (widest page sets the width, narrower pages centred), via a new
  `renderPagedDocToCanvas` helper that mirrors the proven `exportPageToPng`
  clip-and-background logic. SVG export unions the page rectangles into the
  bounds and draws each page's background rect beneath its elements (solid
  colours exact; gradient/texture/image backgrounds fall back to the page base
  colour). PDF/PPTX were already page-aware and are unchanged. (Bug #137.)
