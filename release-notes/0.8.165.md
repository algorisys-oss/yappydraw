# 0.8.165 — Arrowheads follow the curve they're on (2026-07-31)

## Highlights
- Curved connectors' arrowheads were **rotated away from the line they terminate** — by up to
  45° in exported SVG, and by ~90° on canvas in the architectural drawing style. Both now
  follow the path's actual tangent, measured at **0.000°** error.
- The maths that decides where a connector goes lived in **six** hand-copied places. It now
  lives in one, so canvas and export cannot drift apart again.

## Fixes
- **Exported arrowheads used the bounding-box chord, not the path tangent.** `export.ts` took
  `Math.atan2(el.height, el.width)` — the straight line between the connector's corners —
  while the stroke it drew was a cubic whose default control points are axis-aligned. The
  glyph and the line therefore agreed only when the connector was straight or perfectly
  stacked. Across 101 audited diagrams the worst case was 45°, hit whenever the horizontal
  and vertical offsets were equal.
- **The architectural canvas renderer was worse, and had gone unnoticed.** Alone among the six
  copies it omitted the default-control-point fallback, so with no authored `controlPoints`
  (i.e. every DSL- and API-authored edge — neither `api.connect()` nor the DSL engine ever
  writes them) it collapsed to `cp1 = cp2 = start`. That made the start angle `atan2(0, 0)` —
  **due east**, a ~90° error that fails silently because `atan2` of the zero vector is 0, not
  an error — and the end angle the chord again. Sketch rendering was always correct, which is
  why the original report concluded the bug was export-only.
- **Export ignored a rerouted connector's waypoints**, always spanning the bounding box.
  Bezier and elbow connectors now export through their actual `points`.
- **Elbow connectors could read a zero-length segment** for their angle when two consecutive
  waypoints coincided; the architectural path had no de-duplication at all.
- A control point sitting exactly on its endpoint no longer aims the head due east: the angle
  walk steps along the control polygon to the next distinct point, and falls back to the chord
  only as a last resort.

## Internal / tests / docs
- New `frontend/src/utils/connector-geometry.ts` — one `connectorGeometry(el)` returning the
  endpoints, both **outward** arrowhead angles, the effective control points and the SVG path.
  `renderBezier`, `renderElbow`, `renderStraight`, `renderArchitectural`, `definePath` and the
  SVG exporter all consume it. (`renderFlow` and `estimatePathLength` still carry their own
  copies — both correct, neither arrowhead-related; they're the next adopters.)
- `normalizePoints` moved to a new leaf module `utils/points.ts`, re-exported from
  `render-element` so no caller changes. Without this the geometry helper could not be
  unit-tested — importing it dragged in the shape registry, the render pipeline and roughjs.
- `frontend/src/utils/connector-geometry.test.ts` — 14 `bun:test` cases including the
  default-cp property that the chord violated (exactly ±90° regardless of the off-axis
  offset), the 41.5° regression case, and three degenerate-control-point cases.
- `tests/arrowhead-orientation.spec.ts` — 4 Playwright cases asserting the acceptance criteria
  numerically against the exported SVG: parse each `<polygon>` and the cubic it terminates,
  compare the glyph's axis of symmetry to the curve's tangent.
- `docs/arrowhead-orientation-spec.md` committed, with the review corrections folded in and
  marked **[R]**: the "export-only" framing, the mislabelled aggregate counts ("70 files" was
  70 *glyphs*, across 22 files), and acceptance criteria 3 and 5, which as written could be
  satisfied by a broken renderer and by nothing at all respectively.

## Breaking changes / migration
None. Straight connectors are untouched. Curved connectors keep their path and their arrowhead
*positions*; only the head **rotation** changes — from wrong to right.
