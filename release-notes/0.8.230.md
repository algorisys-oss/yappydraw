# 0.8.230 — refund, delivery and contact pages (2026-09-03)

## Features

- **Three new policy pages**, required by Razorpay before international payments can be
  enabled on an account, and useful regardless:

  - **`/contact.html`** — registered and operating address, phone, support and general email,
    response times, and where to raise a bug.
  - **`/refund-policy.html`** — Refund & Cancellation Policy, split by what was actually paid.
  - **`/delivery-policy.html`** — Shipping & Delivery Policy, written honestly for a digital
    service: nothing is shipped, and what is delivered is stated with timeframes.

  All three match the existing legal pages, are excluded from the service worker's navigation
  fallback so they cannot be replaced by the app shell, and are linked from the status bar in
  all five languages, the prerendered page footers, and `/founders/`.

- **The refund policy distinguishes the two payments**, because they are not the same thing:

  | | Refundable |
  |---|---|
  | Voluntary support | No. It is a contribution, not a purchase. Duplicates and obvious mistakes are refunded in full within 7 days. |
  | Founding Supporter | Full refund within 7 days, no reason required. After that, only if we have failed to deliver. |

  A founding place promises specific things in return, so a blanket no-refund rule would be
  both weak under consumer law and at odds with what the page says about itself. Nothing in
  the policy limits statutory rights.

## Internal

- The `/founders/` page now carries its own refunds-and-delivery summary linking both policies,
  so a payer sees the terms where they are deciding rather than only in a footer.
