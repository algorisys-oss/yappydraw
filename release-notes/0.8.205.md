# 0.8.205 — Sketch corners that actually meet (2026-08-20)

Reported with a screen recording: a shape in Sketch style, zoomed in, with a visible step at every
corner — one edge overshooting the corner, the next starting short of it, and a square nib of
background showing through on the outside of the turn. *"Is it supposed to be this way at the
ends?"*

Partly yes — RoughJS is deliberately imprecise, that is the whole point of a hand-drawn look — and
partly no. The wobble belongs in the middle of an edge, not at its ends.

## Highlights

- **Sketch corners meet.** Every shape drawn in Sketch style closes its corners, at every
  sloppiness, at every zoom.
- **Sloppiness starts at 0.** It used to start at 1 — on a drawing style where it does nothing.

## Fixes

### The ends of every sketch edge were in the wrong place

`buildRenderOptions` never set RoughJS's `preserveVertices`, so the endpoints of each edge were
jittered independently and adjacent edges stopped sharing a corner. Measured on the reported shape
(130 × 102 parallelogram), distance from the true corner to the nearest point actually drawn:

| Sloppiness | corner error | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 | 0.43 px | 0.00 px |
| 1 | 0.85 px | 0.00 px |
| 2 | 1.71 px | 0.00 px |

Sub-pixel in world units — but sketch geometry is generated in world space and then scaled by the
view, so at the ~5× zoom in the recording it was ~4 px against a ~20 px stroke. `preserveVertices`
pins both ends of every edge to the real vertex and leaves the wobble in the middle, which is
where the hand-drawn character comes from anyway.

### Butt caps left a wedge at thick corners

Even with both ends in the same place, RoughJS strokes each edge as its own line, and the canvas
default is `lineCap: butt` with no join — so two thick edges meeting at an angle left a square nib
of background showing on the outside of the turn. The sketch pass now draws with round caps and
joins, which also reads more like a pen than a cut ribbon.

### Sloppiness defaulted to 1 on a style that ignores it

The default `roughness` was 1 in three places while the default drawing style is `architectural`,
where roughness has no effect. The panel therefore showed Sloppiness 1 on shapes that render
perfectly clean, and choosing Sketch silently applied it. New shapes start at 0; turn it up when
you want the wobble.

## Internal / tests / docs

- `frontend/src/shapes/base/sketch-corners.test.ts` — asserts the GEOMETRY, not the flag: it
  builds the real render options, generates the drawable and measures every declared vertex
  against the nearest point drawn. Fails at 0.43 px with the fix reverted.
- Applies to every sketch shape at once — all of them route through `buildRenderOptions` and the
  `renderSketch` dispatch — plus the two appearance-stack `rc.path` calls and the variable-width
  ribbon.
- 1,024 unit tests.

## Breaking changes / migration

None. Every saved element carries its own `roughness`, and the migration default for elements that
predate the field stays at 1, so existing drawings keep exactly the look they were drawn with —
only NEW shapes start at 0. Corners in existing sketch drawings will close, which is the fix.
