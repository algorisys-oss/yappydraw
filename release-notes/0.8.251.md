# 0.8.251 — Scene Timeline in the menu (2026-09-14)

## Highlights
- **Menu → Panels → Scene Timeline.** The timeline that plays and loops a whole composition had
  no menu entry or shortcut until now. The only ways to open it were a button in the Stick Figure
  panel or `Yappy.toggleSceneTimeline(true)` in the console.

## Features
- New menu item under **Panels**, right below Keyframes, with a check mark while the timeline is
  open.
- Not offered in animation documents, where the frame timeline replaces it.
- Label translated into German, Spanish, French and Japanese.

## Fixes
- **Only one timeline at a time.** Opening the Scene Timeline now closes the Keyframes panel,
  just as opening Keyframes already closed the Scene Timeline. Before, opening them in that order
  left both open and driving one shared playhead.
- **The help said "Menu → View" in twelve places, and the menu has no View group.** All twelve
  references now say **Menu → Panels**: Keyframes, Show Dimensions, Teaching Mode, Swatches,
  Graphic Styles, History, Symbols, Measure Tool, Pathfinder Strip and Add Adjustment Layer.

## Internal / tests / docs
- `tests/scene-timeline-menu.spec.ts` (3 tests) clicks through the real menu instead of calling
  the toggle. It covers open/close with the check mark, closing Keyframes, and the item's absence
  in animation documents.
- The existing keyframe-panel, scene-playback, scene-duration and composition-evaluator specs
  still pass.
- The Animation and Stick Animation help docs now say where to open the Scene Timeline.

## Breaking changes / migration
None.
