Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Nothing Is Shipped

YappyDraw is a digital service delivered entirely over the internet. No physical goods are sold, so there is no shipping, no courier, no delivery address and no shipping cost, in India or anywhere else.

What Is Delivered, and When

YappyDraw itself is free and needs no delivery. Open yappydraw.com in any modern browser and it works immediately. No account, no installation and no payment is required. It can also be installed as a web app and used offline.

For a Founding Supporter place, delivery is as follows:

  • Immediately. Razorpay confirms the payment on screen and emails you a receipt.
  • Within 2 working days. We email you at the address used at checkout with your founder confirmation and an invitation to the founder community.
  • Ongoing. Early access to new features, roadmap voting and community access continue from that point for as long as YappyDraw is developed.

A voluntary support payment delivers nothing, by design. It is a contribution rather than a purchase, and Razorpay's emailed receipt is your record of it.

If Something Does Not Arrive

If you have paid and not heard from us within 2 working days, check your spam folder first, then email support@algorisys.com with the payment date and the email address you used. We will sort it out.

If we cannot deliver what you paid for, you are entitled to a full refund under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

International

The service is delivered over the internet and is available worldwide, subject to any applicable laws in your country. Because nothing physical is shipped, there are no customs duties, import charges or international delivery timelines.
